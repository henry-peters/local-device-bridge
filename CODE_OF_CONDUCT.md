# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting. Project maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository’s security/contact channel.
